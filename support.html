Frequently Asked Questions

How does live availability work?
QuickVolt pulls real-time charger status from Google's aggregated station network, so you can see which chargers are currently open, full, or unavailable.

Why does a station say "Live data unavailable"?
Some stations, including many Tesla Superchargers, don't share live port status with apps like QuickVolt, even though Google Maps itself may show it. For those stations, tap "See live status in Google Maps" on the station's details screen to check the current availability there.

Do I need an account to use QuickVolt?
No. You can browse and search as a guest. Creating an account lets you save favorites, leave reviews, and get notified when a queued charger frees up.

How do I delete my account?
Open the app, go to your Account tab, and tap "Delete Account." This permanently removes your account and associated data.

How does "Notify me when free" work?
Queue a full station from its detail page, and you'll get a push notification the moment a port opens up, even if the app is closed.

Is my location data shared with anyone?
Your location is used only to show nearby chargers and is not sold to third parties.
