About

About QuickVolt

The idea started with a familiar frustration. A charger that showed as available online, only to pull up and find every port occupied or broken, with no way to have known before making the drive. After that happened one too many times, I decided to build something that shows the truth in real time instead of a stale listing.

It's a solo project. I built the map, the live availability system, and the notification backend that watches queued chargers myself, under the name Hashir Butt.

It's free, and you don't need an account to search or browse. Sign up only if you want to save favorites or leave a review.

Hashir Butt · Founder & sole developer, QuickVolt

Privacy Policy

Last updated January 2026

Information We Collect

QuickVolt collects only what it needs to show you nearby chargers and their live status: your approximate device location while the app is open, your chosen EV connector type, and, if you create an account, your email and any favorites or reviews you save.

How We Use Your Information

Location is used to find and rank chargers near you. Your connector preference filters results to stations you can actually use. If you queue a station, we use your device's push token to notify you when a port opens up.

Guest Mode

You can search and browse without an account. No personal information is collected unless you choose to sign up.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information or location data to third parties. Charger status data is pulled from Google's aggregated charging network to show live availability.

Data Retention & Deletion

Account data is kept only while your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and associated data at any time from Account, then Delete Account, inside the app.

Children's Privacy

QuickVolt is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
