Why QuickVolt

Most charger apps either only show you one network's own stations, or dump every charger on a map with no way to tell which ones you can actually use.

Every Charger, One Map

Apps built by a single network only show their own stations. QuickVolt pulls live status from Google's charger network instead, so ChargePoint, EVgo, Tesla, whatever's actually near you shows up.

Built for Your Exact Car

Pick your EV model once. QuickVolt only shows chargers with a connector that actually fits, so you're not finding out it's the wrong plug after you've already driven there.

A Real Notification, Not a Guess

Queue a full station and QuickVolt checks it in the background. The moment a port opens up, you get a push notification, even with the app closed.

About QuickVolt

The idea came from a road trip to visit my grandmother two summers ago. I pulled into a charging station that showed up as open on the map I was using, only to find all four ports either occupied or broken, with no way to have known that before driving fifteen minutes out of my way. I sat in that parking lot for almost an hour waiting for a spot to open up, watching other drivers pull in and turn right back around. That was the moment I decided to build something that actually tells you the truth before you get there.

It's a solo project. I built the map, the live availability system, and the notification backend that watches queued chargers myself, under the name Hashir Butt.

It's free, and you don't need an account to search or browse. Sign up only if you want to save favorites or leave a review.
